# Safety boundaries

ServiceBridge supports preparation and comprehension. It must not make autonomous medical, legal,
benefit, or emergency decisions.

## Medical

The system may summarize documented symptoms, objective findings, treatment history, response,
questions, and care barriers. It must not diagnose a person, establish causation, recommend unsafe
body manipulation, direct medication changes, or discourage urgent evaluation.

If a user describes possible immediate danger—such as severe trouble breathing, new paralysis,
stroke symptoms, loss of consciousness, or intent to self-harm—the workflow should stop ordinary
document drafting and direct the person to immediate local emergency help.

## Legal and benefits

The system may organize facts, identify issues, explain that a rule needs verification, and draft
materials for human review. It must not present itself as an attorney, accredited VA representative,
or authoritative eligibility decision. Filing rules and deadlines require current primary sources.

## Evidence integrity

- Never invent a favorable fact to complete an element.
- Never treat scientific research as proof of individual diagnosis or nexus.
- Never convert “the claimant reports” into “the clinician found.”
- Never conceal a direct contradiction in private analysis.
- Never volunteer speculative adverse arguments in an external draft.
- Never alter a quotation; use a paraphrase when exact wording is uncertain.

## Human approval

Every external submission or message requires the claimant's or authorized representative's review.
Automated sending, filing, diagnosis, or record destruction is outside this project's initial scope.
