ServiceBridge Advocate is an early-stage technology and social-impact venture. This document
states the project's current intent regarding intellectual property, licensing, business structure,
and future development. It is informational and may be revised as the venture evolves.

## Intellectual-property strategy

ServiceBridge may protect qualifying intellectual property through the mechanisms appropriate
to the particular asset, including:

- trademarks and service marks for names, logos, branding, and source identifiers;
- copyright for original software, documentation, designs, written content, and other
  copyrightable works;
- patents or patent applications for qualifying inventions, systems, or methods where useful
  and legally available;
- trade-secret and confidentiality practices for non-public know-how, methods, data,
  operational processes, and proprietary technology; and
- commercial, research, open-source, dual-license, or other licensing arrangements.

No statement in this repository should be read as a representation that any particular idea,
feature, workflow, method, or invention is patented, patentable, registered as a trademark, or
otherwise protected unless that status is stated expressly.

## Product and policy evolution

ServiceBridge may revise, expand, discontinue, or reorganize its products, services, policies,
pricing, technical architecture, data practices, partnerships, or market focus as the project
develops, subject to applicable law and existing contractual or license obligations.

Public roadmaps, prototypes, demonstrations, research plans, and feature descriptions are
forward-looking development materials rather than promises that a particular feature or
business model will remain unchanged.

## Organizational flexibility

ServiceBridge may operate through or form one or more appropriate organizational structures,
including an LLC, corporation, public-benefit entity, affiliated nonprofit, subsidiary, partnership,
or other lawful structure.

The venture may pursue a combination of:

- commercial subscriptions and software licensing;
- subsidized or no-cost access programs;
- grants and philanthropic support;
- veteran and disability entrepreneurship programs;
- research collaborations;
- government procurement and contracting;
- strategic partnerships;
- non-dilutive technology credits and accelerator programs; and
- angel, pre-seed, venture, or other investment.

The use of one pathway does not commit ServiceBridge to remain exclusively within that funding
or organizational model.

## Social-impact mission

ServiceBridge's current mission is to reduce the administrative and evidentiary burden faced by
veterans, people with disabilities, caregivers, advocates, clinicians, and organizations navigating
complex medical, disability-benefit, accommodation, and related systems.

The project may use commercial revenue to support that mission while exploring subsidized,
nonprofit, government, research, and philanthropic pathways that can expand access.

rights outside the rights expressly granted by the applicable open-source license or another
written agreement.

Questions about licensing, partnerships, research, or commercial use can be addressed through
the repository's normal contact channels.
