# Benefit lanes

Different programs ask different questions. ServiceBridge treats mixing their standards as a
high-risk error.

| Lane | Central question | Frequent failure the policy guards against |
|---|---|---|
| VA service connection | Is a current disability linked to qualifying service by a supported theory? | Treating Guard membership as one continuous active-duty period—or ignoring documented training periods |
| VA rating | How severe is an already service-connected disability under current criteria? | Confusing nexus evidence with severity evidence |
| TDIU/SMC | What do service-connected disabilities do to employment or special-compensation criteria? | Using all impairments without separating service-connected effects |
| SSDI/SSI | Can medically determinable impairments permit sustained work under the governing standard? | Importing VA causation rules instead of analyzing function and duration |
| State disability | Does the person meet the state's specific medical, procedural, and financial rules? | Assuming federal or VA approval controls the state decision |
| Education TPD | Does the person meet the controlling loan program's discharge standard? | Treating federal, private, and state loans as one program |
| ADA/504 | What limitation requires what effective modification? | Oversharing diagnosis while failing to explain the requested accommodation |
| Clinical advocacy | What is documented, reported, unresolved, urgent, and worth asking next? | Declaring a disputed diagnosis or mechanism proven |

## Guard and Reserve service

The service-connection lane requires a period-by-period ledger. Documents should identify the date,
order or attendance source, authority when available, and whether the period is active duty,
ACDUTRA, INACDUTRA, or unresolved. The system must not decide duty status solely from a portal's
abbreviated “active duty dates.” Current primary authority and the claimant's actual orders control.

## Functional evidence

SSDI, state disability, TDIU, and many accommodation questions depend on function over time. Useful
fields include attendance, persistence, pace, unscheduled breaks, off-task time, positioning,
sitting/standing tolerance, travel, communication, activities of daily living, symptom variability,
and the ability to sustain performance reliably—not merely once on a favorable day.
