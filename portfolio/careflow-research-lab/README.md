# CareFlow Research Lab

**Independent UX Research + Product Study by AJ Kivela**

CareFlow Research Lab is an interview-ready healthcare UX research portfolio project built around a fictional medication-coordination workflow for hospice nurses, care coordinators, and clinical administrators.

It demonstrates a complete research workflow: research planning, participant operations, moderated usability testing, evidence capture, task/confidence metrics, synthesis, evidence traceability, findings, research repository, and recruiter-facing case-study communication.

## Portfolio provenance

- Independent portfolio study; not employment or client work.
- Seeded participants, request data, quotes, metrics, and findings are fictional/simulated.
- No PHI is required or intended.
- The demo separates direct observation from researcher interpretation.
- AI-assisted synthesis is presented as a review aid, not as fabricated evidence.

## Run

Open [index.html](index.html) in a modern browser. The committed demo is self-contained: no install, API key, package manager, or build step is required.

## Skills demonstrated

Research planning; semi-structured interview framework; contextual-inquiry prompts; moderated usability testing; concept evaluation; mixed-method task metrics; research operations; evidence capture and tagging; affinity synthesis; traceability; healthcare workflow reasoning; findings prioritization; research repository design; responsible AI-assisted research governance.

## Quality boundary

The seeded pilot evidence is simulated. It demonstrates process and product-research execution, not completed research with real BetterRX users or a claim of BetterRX employment. Real-participant findings would require recruitment, consent, sessions, analysis, and validation.
