Safety / source boundary

Generated anatomical art and Blender geometry are demonstrative teaching media. They are not diagnostic imaging, measured patient-specific geometry, or a substitute for the original medical record. MedForge should always distinguish visible observations, source-recorded diagnoses, interpretation, mechanism hypothesis, uncertainty, and next verification steps.
