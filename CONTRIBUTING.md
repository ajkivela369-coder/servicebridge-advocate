# Contributing

Contributions that improve accessibility, provenance, record parsing, evaluations, and benefit-lane
separation are welcome.

1. Use synthetic data in tests, issues, screenshots, and pull requests.
2. Do not submit personal medical narratives, claim numbers, service records, or private agency
   correspondence.
3. Add tests for behavior changes.
4. Identify the controlling primary authority for legal-rule changes and record its effective date.
5. Preserve the difference between source fact, reported fact, inference, and unknown.
6. Keep generated drafts subject to human review.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is licensed under the MIT License.
